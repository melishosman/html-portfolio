The Best Movies According to Me

My Favourite 4 cinematic works of all-time.

The Lord of the Rings Movie Series

This story is part of a profound film series that explores how even seemingly insignificant individuals can achieve great things, highlighting the bonds of friendship and sacrifice. With its fairytale atmosphere, impressive visuals, and inspiring story, it will always be my number one.

Shutter Island

Quite mysterious, intriguing... truly a masterpiece.

The Matrix Trilogy

This is a profound and philosophical film series incorporating Plato's allegory of the cave. It's one of my favorite film series, with impressive visuals and a compelling story that perfectly conveys the falsity of the system we live in and how many people are trapped in a virtual world.

Spirited Away

A magnificent anime that captivates with its visuals and story, about greed and the healing power of love.
